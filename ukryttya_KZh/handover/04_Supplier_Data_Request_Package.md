# Supplier Data Request Package (RFI Templates)

**Project:** Fully underground civil shelter, private plot, Rusanivski Sady, Kyiv
**Purpose:** Four ready-to-send Requests for Information (RFI), one per
supplier category. Each asks the supplier for **their own** product data —
it does not specify a final design, and none of the figures below (opening
sizes, airflow, concrete class) are to be treated as confirmed design
values. They are the client's current *indicative* concept, explicitly
labeled as such, so suppliers can propose real products against something
concrete. All four are subject to revision once the licensed structural
engineer assigns a protection class (see Design Basis & Open Issues
Register, item OI-04) and completes calculations (OI-05).

---

## RFI-1: Protective / Sealed Entrance Door

**To:** [door supplier name]
**From:** [client name / representative]
**Subject:** Data request — protective and sealed doors for a private
underground civil shelter

We are planning a fully underground civil shelter and are gathering
supplier data before finalizing the design. Please treat all figures below
as **indicative only** — we need your product's actual specifications so
our engineer can finalize the opening and embedded parts around your real
product, not the other way around.

**Indicative opening (subject to change):** approx. 900 mm wide rough
opening; wall thickness approx. 300 mm reinforced concrete.

**Please provide for your standard product range suitable for a private
underground shelter:**

1. Available protection classes / rated overpressure (kPa) and the
   standard(s) each is certified against.
2. Door leaf and frame dimensions, and the exact rough-opening / embedded
   frame dimensions your product requires.
3. Weight of the complete assembly (for lintel/jamb design).
4. Anchoring / embedded-parts requirements (bolt pattern, embedment depth,
   required concrete cover).
5. Whether you offer both an outer (protective/blast-rated) door and an
   inner airtight/sealed door as a matched pair, and their combined
   installation depth requirement (for our vestibule sizing).
6. Sealing/gasket system and expected service life / maintenance interval.
7. Lead time and current indicative pricing for budgeting purposes only
   (not a purchase order).

## RFI-2: Emergency Exit Hatch

**To:** [hatch supplier name]
**From:** [client name / representative]
**Subject:** Data request — emergency exit hatch for a private underground
civil shelter

**Indicative opening (subject to change):** approx. 800 × 800 mm clear
opening through a 400 mm reinforced-concrete slab, at the top of a vertical
escape shaft.

**Please provide:**

1. Standard hatch sizes available and the exact rough-opening dimension
   each requires.
2. Frame/embedded-part details (steel frame dimensions, anchoring into
   concrete, required cover).
3. Rated load (pedestrian / soil surcharge above, if buried under
   landscaping) and any waterproofing/sealing certification.
4. Locking/release mechanism suitable for emergency egress from inside,
   and whether it can be operated by a single person.
5. Recommended ladder/rung system compatible with your hatch, if you offer
   one, or clearance dimensions needed to fit a third-party ladder.
6. Lead time and current indicative pricing for budgeting purposes only.

## RFI-3: Ventilation Equipment

**To:** [ventilation supplier name]
**From:** [client name / representative]
**Subject:** Data request — ventilation for a private underground civil
shelter, 4 occupants

We would like your recommendation, not ours — please size equipment
based on your own methodology for a sealed underground shelter for 4
occupants, and tell us what you need from us to do that (room volume,
occupancy schedule, whether NBC/CBRN filtration is required, etc.).

**Please provide, for both a basic mechanical-ventilation option and a
filtered/NBC-capable option:**

1. Your recommended airflow rate and the standard/guideline you size it
   against.
2. Equipment list (fan(s), filters, valves — including blast/overpressure
   valves if relevant to a filtered option), dimensions, and power
   consumption of each.
3. Duct/sleeve diameters required through the concrete envelope, for how
   many penetrations (supply/exhaust), and any manual backup (hand-crank)
   options.
4. Required sensors/interlocks you recommend (CO₂, CO, smoke, or others)
   and whether these are included or third-party.
5. Maintenance interval and filter replacement schedule/cost.
6. Lead time and current indicative pricing for budgeting purposes only.

## RFI-4: Concrete Supply

**To:** [concrete plant / supplier name]
**From:** [client name / representative]
**Subject:** Data request — waterproof concrete mix for a private
underground civil shelter

We are unable to apply external membrane waterproofing on this structure,
so we are exploring an integral waterproof-concrete ("white box") approach
and need your plant's actual mix design and guidance, not a generic
assumption.

**Please provide:**

1. Your standard mix designs available at strength/grade in the general
   range of C35/45 (or your closest equivalent), with water-penetration
   (W-class or equivalent) and freeze-thaw (F-class or equivalent) ratings
   you can certify.
2. Any integral waterproofing admixture (e.g., crystalline) systems you
   offer or recommend for a fully buried structure without membrane
   waterproofing, and technical literature/certification for them.
3. Waterstop and joint-sealing products you supply or recommend for
   construction/movement joints, compatible with your mix.
4. Pumping/delivery capability to a private plot in Rusanivski Sady, Kyiv
   (truck-mixer access, pump availability), and typical batch sizes.
5. Lead time from order to delivery, and current indicative pricing per m³
   for budgeting purposes only (final quantities to be confirmed by the
   structural engineer's issued-for-construction drawings — not by this
   request).

## Notes for all four RFIs

- Do **not** commit to a purchase order based on these responses alone.
- Forward all supplier responses to the licensed structural engineer
  (see Engineer Handover Sheet, Doc.02) — embedded parts, openings, and
  reinforcement around openings must be finalized against the *actual*
  product dimensions received here, not the indicative figures above.
- Track responses in the Design Basis & Open Issues Register (Doc.01),
  closing OI-06 (door/hatch/ventilation) and OI-07 (concrete) once
  supplier data is in hand and reviewed by the engineer.
