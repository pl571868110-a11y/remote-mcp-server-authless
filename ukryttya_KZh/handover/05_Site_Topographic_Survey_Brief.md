# Site Survey / Topographic Survey Brief

**Project:** Fully underground civil shelter, private plot, Rusanivski Sady, Kyiv
**Purpose of this brief:** to be issued to a licensed surveyor as a
scope-of-work request. It defines *what to establish*, not the answers —
no site levels, boundaries, or clearances are assumed here.

---

## 1. Objective

Establish the physical and legal constraints of the plot so the licensed
structural engineer can site the structure, set excavation depth relative
to real ground level, and identify any conflicts before design proceeds.

## 2. Information requested (deliverables from the surveyor)

| # | Item | Why it's needed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Topographic survey of the plot (existing ground levels, contours) tied to a recognized datum | Establishes actual ground level referenced by the design (the earlier reference-concept package assumed a generic ground level with no survey backing it) |
| 2 | Plot boundaries and any setback/easement lines | Confirms the structure and its excavation can legally fit on the plot |
| 3 | Location, depth, and type of existing underground utilities (water, sewer, electrical, gas, communications) crossing or near the plot | Avoids utility conflicts during excavation; may affect entrance/vent/cable routing |
| 4 | Location and foundation depth (if known/obtainable) of neighboring structures within an influence zone of the excavation | Assesses risk of ground movement affecting neighbors, and vice versa |
| 5 | Existing trees, structures, paving, fences, or other features on the plot that may affect access or excavation | Construction planning |
| 6 | Access route and any width/height/load restrictions for excavation equipment, concrete trucks, and a concrete pump | Determines feasible construction method and equipment |
| 7 | Any existing groundwater, drainage, or flooding history known locally (informal, to complement — not substitute for — the geotechnical investigation) | Cross-check for the geotechnical brief (Doc.03) |
| 8 | Confirmation of legal permission/registration status for the intended structure, if within the surveyor's scope, or a note on which authority to approach | Avoids proceeding on an unpermittable structure |

## 3. Deliverable format requested

- A signed topographic/site survey drawing (plan, with levels) tied to a
  stated datum, plus a written note on utilities, access constraints, and
  neighboring structures as available.

## 4. Explicitly not requested from this brief

- No structural siting decision, excavation depth recommendation, or
  setback design — those are the structural engineer's responsibility,
  using this survey as an input, in conjunction with the geotechnical
  report (Doc.03).

## 5. Relationship to other open issues

Closes **OI-02** in the Design Basis & Open Issues Register (Doc.01).
Results should be forwarded to the licensed structural engineer alongside
the geotechnical report before any siting or excavation-depth decision is
finalized.
