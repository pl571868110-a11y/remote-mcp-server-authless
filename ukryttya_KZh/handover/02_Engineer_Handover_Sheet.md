## 1. Purpose of this handover

This sheet accompanies the documents listed below when the project is
handed to a licensed structural engineer for the actual design. It exists
so the engineer starts from a clear, honest baseline: **nothing enclosed is
a design.** It is a concept brief plus a list of what is still missing.

## 3. What is explicitly NOT enclosed (and must not be assumed)

- A geotechnical report (not yet commissioned — see item 3 above)
- A topographic/site survey (not yet commissioned — see item 5 above)
- Any structural calculation (walls, slabs, uplift/UPL, lateral earth pressure)
- An assigned civil-defense protection class
- Confirmed supplier data for doors, emergency hatch, ventilation, or concrete mix
- Any reinforcement, concrete quantity, or budget figure that may be relied upon

## 4. What is requested from the engineer

1. Review the Design Basis & Open Issues Register (Doc.01) and confirm, add
   to, or revise the open issues as you see fit — this register is a
   starting point, not a constraint on your professional judgment.
2. Advise the client on which investigations/surveys must be commissioned
   before you can begin calculations (Docs. 03 and 05 are drafted for this
   purpose but should be reviewed/amended by you or a suitably qualified
   geotechnical/survey professional before being issued).
3. Determine the applicable civil-defense protection class and the code
   editions in force, and record both in the register.
4. Once OI-01…OI-08 (or your revised list) are closed, produce the
   structural calculation package and issued-for-construction (IFC)
   drawings under your own seal/registration.
