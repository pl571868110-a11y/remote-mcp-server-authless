# Geotechnical Investigation Brief

**Project:** Fully underground civil shelter, private plot, Rusanivski Sady, Kyiv
**Purpose of this brief:** to be issued to a licensed geotechnical
investigation contractor as a scope-of-work request. It defines *what to
find out*, not the answers — no soil parameters are assumed or estimated
here.

**Note:** this brief should be reviewed and, if needed, amended by the
licensed structural engineer or a geotechnical professional before being
sent to a contractor — particularly the number, depth, and location of
test points, which should reflect their judgment and any local geological
records they hold for the Rusanivski Sady area.

---

## 1. Site

- Location: Kyiv, Rusanivski Sady, private plot (exact cadastral boundary
  and address to be supplied by client).
- Proposed structure: fully underground, single-story reinforced-concrete
  box, approximate footprint on the order of a few tens of square meters,
  founded at an approximate depth of ~3 m below existing ground level
  (to be confirmed once a topographic survey — Doc.05 — establishes actual
  ground level).
- Known constraints: private residential plot; proximity to existing
  structures, trees, and underground utilities is not yet documented (see
  Doc.05).

## 2. Objective

Provide the data needed for a licensed structural engineer to:

1. Design the foundation slab and check bearing capacity.
2. Check the structure against uplift/flotation (buoyancy) under
   groundwater loading.
3. Determine lateral earth pressure on the below-grade walls.
4. Assess groundwater control / dewatering needs during construction.
5. Assess soil aggressivity toward concrete and steel reinforcement.

## 3. Information requested (deliverables from the geotechnical contractor)

| # | Item | Why it's needed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Soil stratigraphy / lithology to a depth sufficient to cover the founding level plus an adequate margin (contractor to propose, typically several meters below the anticipated slab underside) | Bearing design, excavation planning |
| 2 | Groundwater level — including seasonal high-water estimate if available | Uplift/UPL check, waterproofing strategy, dewatering |
| 3 | Unit weight (bulk and saturated) of relevant soil layers | Lateral earth pressure, uplift calculation |
| 4 | Angle of internal friction (and cohesion, if relevant) of relevant soil layers | Lateral earth pressure calculation |
| 5 | Allowable/design bearing resistance (R0 or equivalent per applicable code) at the anticipated founding depth | Foundation slab design |
| 6 | Soil and groundwater aggressivity toward concrete and steel (sulfates, chlorides, pH, etc.) | Concrete durability class, reinforcement cover, waterproofing detailing |
| 7 | Recommendations on excavation support, slope stability, and dewatering method for the anticipated depth | Construction method statement, cost estimating |
| 8 | Any evidence of made ground, organic soils, or other conditions requiring special foundation treatment | Risk flag for the structural engineer |

## 4. Suggested scope of investigation (for contractor to confirm/revise)

- Number and location of boreholes or test pits: to be proposed by the
  geotechnical contractor based on plot size and local geology; at minimum
  enough points to characterize variability across the footprint.
- In-situ testing (e.g., SPT, CPT, or equivalent) as appropriate to the
  contractor's standard practice.
- Laboratory testing of recovered samples for classification, strength,
  and chemical aggressivity.
- Groundwater monitoring: at least one reading at time of drilling; a
  standpipe/piezometer installation is recommended if seasonal variation
  is a concern, given the "worst case = GWL at ground level" scenario
  flagged as a risk in the design basis register.

## 5. Deliverable format requested

- A signed/sealed geotechnical investigation report suitable for use by a
  licensed structural engineer, including borehole logs, laboratory
  results, groundwater observations, and engineering recommendations
  (bearing resistance, lateral pressure parameters, aggressivity class).

## 6. Explicitly not requested from this brief

- No structural design, slab thickness, or reinforcement recommendation —
  that is the structural engineer's responsibility, using this report as
  an input.
