## 1. Purpose and effect of this register

This register records:

1. The **design basis** — what is known/assumed about the project, and on
   what authority (client brief vs. assumption vs. confirmed survey/test data).
2. The **open issues** — the specific inputs and calculations that are
   currently missing, and that **block** any drawing, quantity, or budget
   figure from being treated as project data.

Until every item in Section 3 is closed (status changed from OPEN to
CLOSED, with the closing document referenced), **no geometry, reinforcement
quantity, concrete quantity, budget figure, or KZh-numbered drawing may be
issued, quoted from, procured against, or built from.** This applies
retroactively to the reference-concept package generated earlier in this
session.

Only a licensed structural engineer (or a geotechnical/survey professional
for their respective disciplines) may close an item or issue a superseding
calculation, drawing, or quantity.
