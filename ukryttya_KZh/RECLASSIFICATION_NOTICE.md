**Status of everything previously generated in this folder
(`ukryttya_3x4_KZh_draft.pdf`, `ukryttya_3x4_full_estimate_2026.xlsx`,
`png/`, `svg/`, and all figures quoted in `README_KZh.md`):**

> ## REFERENCE CONCEPT ONLY — NOT FOR DESIGN, PROCUREMENT OR CONSTRUCTION

Per the approved **Design Basis & Open Issues Register Rev.01** (see
`handover/01_Design_Basis_and_Open_Issues_Register_Rev01.md`), all geometry,
reinforcement quantities, concrete quantities, budgets, and KZh-numbered
drawings generated earlier in this session are **frozen** and superseded
pending resolution of the blocking inputs and structural calculations listed
in that register.

Concretely, this means:

- **KZh-0 … KZh-17** in `ukryttya_3x4_KZh_draft.pdf` (and the matching files
  in `png/` / `svg/`) are **not design drawings**. They illustrate a concept
  layout only and must not be issued, quoted, or built from.
- The **26.0 m³ concrete**, **2.33 t reinforcement**, and all **LOW /
  REALISTIC / HIGH budget figures** in
  `ukryttya_3x4_full_estimate_2026.xlsx` and `README_KZh.md` are **not
  project quantities**. They are not to be used for procurement, tendering,
  or budgeting decisions.
- The uplift (buoyancy / UPL), lateral earth pressure, slab, and
  reinforcement narratives in the prior package are **not engineering
  conclusions**. No load-bearing, waterproofing, or protection-class
  decision may rely on them.
- None of the above files have been regenerated or revised as part of this
  reclassification — they are left as-is, only their **status** changes.

**What supersedes them going forward** is the handover package in
`handover/`:

1. `01_Design_Basis_and_Open_Issues_Register_Rev01.md`

No new dimensions, reinforcement, concrete quantities, or budget values are
introduced in this reclassification or in the handover package. Those will
only be produced by, or under the direct supervision of, a licensed
structural engineer once the open issues below are closed.
